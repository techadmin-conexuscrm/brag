Brand

The idea behind Conexus.

The name

Con·nect + nexus — the point where connections converge. Conexus is where customer relationships come together and move.

What it is

A multi-tenant CRM platform that unifies calls, messages, orders and pipeline for modern teams — one connected place to run customer relationships end to end.

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space of at least ½× the logo height on all sides. Minimum: full logo 150px wide, mark 24px. In tiny spaces use the mark, not the full logo.

Don’t stretch, recolor, rotate, add effects, place on low-contrast backgrounds, or use the dark-background logo on light. Always use the supplied files.
