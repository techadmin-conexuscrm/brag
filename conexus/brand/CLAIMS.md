# Conexus — Approved Claims

Every Conexus video, caption and post uses claims from this file only, worded exactly as
written. If a claim isn't here, it doesn't go on screen until it is verified and added.

## Compliance & security (approved wording — 2026-10-31)

On-screen line:

> **PCI DSS (self-assessed) · HIPAA-ready · GDPR-aligned · SOC 2 Type I & ISO 27001 in progress**

| Standard | Status | Say | Never say |
|---|---|---|---|
| PCI DSS | Self-assessment completed | "PCI DSS self-assessed" | "PCI Level 1/2/4", "PCI certified" |
| HIPAA | Ready | "HIPAA-ready" | "HIPAA certified", "HIPAA compliant" (no such certification exists) |
| GDPR | Aligned | "GDPR-aligned" | "GDPR compliant", "GDPR certified" |
| SOC 2 Type I | Auditor engaged, audit in progress | "SOC 2 Type I in progress" | "SOC 2 certified", "SOC 2 compliant" (until the report is issued) |
| ISO 27001 | In progress | "ISO 27001 in progress" | "ISO 27001 certified" (until certified) |

When a status changes (SOC 2 report issued, ISO 27001 certificate granted), update this table
first, then re-render any video that shows the line.

Open question: will Conexus sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with healthcare clients?
"HIPAA-ready" is only credible if yes.

## Numbers / ROI

No savings or revenue figures go on screen unless they are either:
1. a real, documented client result (with the client's permission), or
2. a calculation from published list prices, with the assumptions shown on screen.
