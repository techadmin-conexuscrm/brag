# Video 4 — "Nobody forgets what they're owed"

- **Angle:** the payday question every call-center floor knows ("did my spiff count?") — answered by the product, automatically.
- **Hook (0–2s):** a real-looking team-chat message: "Hey — did my spiff from Tuesday count?"
- **Highlights:** (1) spiff credits the moment the sale closes, (2) leaderboard everyone can see, (3) ledger with every dollar.
- **Punchline:** "Payday math, done." → end card.
- **Tone:** polished, confident, a little warm. Clean cuts through the background, no flashy effects.
- **Format:** 1080×1350 (4:5, LinkedIn feed), 30fps, 22s. Captions are the headline on every scene (works muted).
- **Visual identity:** Conexus brand kit — Geist / Geist Mono, Surface #F8FAFC, Ink #0F172A, Conexus Blue #2563EB accent, Success #059669 for credits, Navy #0F2288 end card with reversed-white logo. Real product details: Spiffs table labels ("Flat / Per Sale", Reward, Deadline), the app's gold/silver/bronze medal icons and 1st/2nd/3rd place accent colors.
- **Data:** all names, order numbers and amounts are fictional stand-ins (Jordan T., Priya S., Maya R., Luis M.). Ledger rows sum to the balance shown ($175.00).
- **Claims used (FEATURES.md):** spiffs credit automatically on completed sales; running balance + ledger; admin adjustments; leaderboard with medals, top performer, most improved.

## Music
120 BPM (beat = 0.5s), A minor → F → C → G, soft kick / hat / bass / pad / pluck. Every cut and entrance on the beat grid. In-key chime on each credit, soft ticks on card entrances, low swells into cuts.
